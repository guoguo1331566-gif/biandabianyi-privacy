常见问题

1. 如何启用键盘？

  • 打开「设置 → 通用 → 键盘 → 键盘 → 添加新键盘」，选择「边打边译 / TransBoard」。
  • 在输入框里长按地球键（或长按键盘左下角的「中/英」键），即可切换到本输入法。

2. 为什么需要开启「完全访问」？

「完全访问」仅用于两件事：调用系统翻译服务，以及在设备本地读写本 App 与键盘之间的共享容器。 本 App 与键盘不发起任何网络请求，输入内容不会离开你的设备。

3. 翻译按钮没有反应 / 提示需要下载语言包？

翻译由系统端侧翻译框架完成，首次使用某个语言方向时，系统需要下载对应语言包。 可打开系统自带「翻译」App 提前下载，或在 App 内按提示请求安装；语言包下载完成后即可离线翻译。

4. 键盘没有声音 / 没有震动？

iOS 不允许第三方键盘震动。按键音跟随系统设置：「设置 → 声音与触感 → 键盘反馈」中的开关。

5. 如何清除我保存的输入习惯与缓存？

本 App「更多」页面提供一键清空入口，会清除本地词库、模糊音设置与译文缓存。卸载 App 也会一并清除。

6. 会收集我的输入内容吗？

不会。所有数据只存在你的设备本地，我们不收集、不上传任何输入内容或个人信息。 详见 隐私政策。

FAQ

1. How do I enable the keyboard?

  • Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose "边打边译 / TransBoard".
  • In any text field, long-press the globe key (or the 中/英 key at the bottom-left) to switch to it.

2. Why does it ask for "Full Access"?

Full Access is used for exactly two things: calling the system translation service, and reading/writing the shared container between this app and its keyboard, locally on your device. The app and keyboard make no network requests; your input never leaves your device.

3. The translate button does nothing / it asks for a language pack.

Translation runs on-device via Apple's translation framework. The first time you use a language pair the system needs to download that language pack. You can pre-download it in the built-in Translate app, or follow the prompt inside this app. Once downloaded, translation works offline.

4. No sound or vibration on the keyboard?

iOS does not allow third-party keyboards to vibrate. Key sounds follow the system setting under Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Keyboard Feedback.

5. How do I clear my saved typing data and cache?

The "More" page in this app provides a one-tap reset that clears the local dictionary, fuzzy-pinyin settings and translation cache. Deleting the app also removes them.

6. Do you collect my typing?

No. All data stays local on your device. We do not collect or upload any input content or personal information. See the Privacy Policy.
